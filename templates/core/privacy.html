Privacy

Reading StatsTricks360 needs no account. An account only keeps your progress, so that it follows you from one device to another. This page says what is kept, why, and how to take it with you or delete it.

If you only read

Nothing about you is kept on the server. There are no adverts, no trackers and no analytics. Pages you mark done without an account are saved in your own browser only.

If you make an account, we keep

  • Your email address, to sign you in and to send the emails an account needs (confirming the address, resetting a password, confirming a deletion). We send nothing else.
  • Your password, stored only as a one-way hash, which cannot be turned back into it. If you sign in with Google instead, Google tells us your email address and an account number; we never see your Google password and keep no access to your Google account.
  • The name you give, to greet you on your dashboard.
  • That you confirmed you are 18 or over, and when you accepted this notice. Accounts are for adults for now.
  • Your progress: which units you marked done, and when. When unit tests arrive, your answers and scores too.

We do not ask for your phone number, your address, your college or anything else, and we never sell or share what we keep.

Cookies

Two, and only when you sign in or fill in a form: one keeps you signed in, and one protects forms against being submitted from another site. Neither is used for tracking.

Security records

To stop people guessing passwords, a failed sign-in is recorded with the email address tried, the internet address it came from, the browser's name and the time. After five failures, signing in to that email address from that internet address is paused for an hour. The records are removed after that hour, or at once when a sign-in succeeds. Successful sign-ins are not logged.

Who else handles it

The application runs with a hosting company, which stores the database, and sends email through an email delivery service, which sees the address and the message it delivers. Both act only on our instructions. They will be named here before accounts open to the public.

Take it with you, or delete it

  • Download: Download my data gives you everything above, as a JSON file.
  • Delete: Delete my account removes your account, your profile and your progress at once. You get one email confirming it. Test attempts, when there are any, stay in the question statistics with nothing linking them to you.

Questions

Ask on the site's GitHub Issues page. Please do not post your email address or anything else personal there.
